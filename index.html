What you can do

Check classroom availability

Pick a date and time and see which rooms in the College of Computer Studies are free.

Avoid schedule conflicts

The system warns you when your section, your own class, or the room is already booked.

Track approvals

Follow each request from submission to Dean and University Administrator approval, or read the reason it was rejected.

Let students vote

Students of the section tell the approvers if they can attend, so proposals fit real availability.

Who uses RoomSync

Faculty

Search rooms, request makeup classes, view schedules.

University clerk

Manages classrooms and tracks and prints makeup records.

Dean and University Administrator

Review and approve or reject requests.

Students

View schedules and vote on proposed makeup classes.
