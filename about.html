About RoomSync

The problem

Classrooms are shared by many classes, and a makeup class needs a free room, a time when the students have no other class, and approval from university officials. Checking all of this by hand takes time and causes double bookings and missed approvals.

Why we built it

We wanted one place where faculty can see free rooms, where the system catches conflicts before they happen, and where the approval trail is visible to everyone involved.

Objectives

  • Show which classrooms are available for a chosen date and time.
  • Check a section’s schedule before a makeup class is booked.
  • Route each request through the Dean and the University Administrator.
  • Give the university clerk tools to manage rooms and records.
  • Let students view schedules and vote on their availability.

Scope

The first version covers the College of Computer Studies and can grow to other colleges. It runs in the browser with sample data and does not need a server.
